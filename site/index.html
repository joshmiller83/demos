Mini catalog

CSV in, catalog out.

A Stencil web component I built for the Urban Institute's Lyndon design system. Point it at a CSV and it renders a catalog you can search, filter and page through. Editors at Urban published libraries, directories and data catalogs this way without touching code. Five presets show its modes, and you can edit a CSV or load your own.
